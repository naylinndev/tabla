# Code of conduct

Tabla is built by and for people who care about good tools. To keep this a
welcoming place for everyone:

- **Be kind and respectful.** Disagree with ideas, not people.
- **Be inclusive.** Everyone is welcome regardless of background, identity,
  experience level or the database they use.
- **Be constructive.** Explain the problem, suggest a way forward, and assume
  good intent.
- **No harassment.** Insults, personal attacks, discriminatory jokes, unwanted
  attention and sharing others' private information are not tolerated.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits or contributions that break these
rules, and may block people who repeatedly do so.

To report a problem privately, use the repository's
[private reporting](https://github.com/naylinndev/tabla/security/advisories/new)
or contact a maintainer through GitHub.
